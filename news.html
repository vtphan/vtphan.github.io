Projects and outreach

  1. 2026

    Polylogue

    Polylogue ↗ is an approach to teaching skills that call for judgment, from weighing evidence to noticing when a friend is struggling, through illustrated, choose-your-path stories. Each student decides how the story goes, sees what that choice costs, and explains why. Then, at a table with classmates who chose differently, each hears their reasons and tries to state the best case for the road not taken. Teachers and researchers co-design the stories with the help of AI; students never interact with an AI.

  2. 2023

    Carpo

    Carpo ↗ is a JupyterLab extension for active learning in data science and machine learning courses. Students work on in-class coding exercises inside Jupyter notebooks and share their code; instructors and teaching assistants look at the shared code and give feedback. It was presented at SIGCSE 2023 with Kritish Pahi.

  3. 2018

    Code4Brownies

    Code4Brownies ↗ is an active learning tool for teaching programming and algorithms. During class, the instructor sends partially complete code to students’ editors, students work on it and share their code back, and the instructor can add hints, run quizzes and polls, and award virtual brownie points. Everything runs from a server on the instructor’s laptop. It was presented at ITiCSE 2018 with Eric Hicks.
