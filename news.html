  2. 2025

    CoAIRS grant

    Dr. Phan and collaborators received a University of Memphis Communities of AI Research Scholars (CoAIRS) award for “Multi-Agent AI for Critical Thinking in Middle School Project-Based Learning” ↗. The team is Vinhthuy Phan, Amy Cook, and Brandon Booth (University of Memphis) with Yeonji Jung (Texas A&M University). CoAIRS is seed funding for interdisciplinary teams from the University’s Center for Emerging Technologies in Artificial Intelligence (CERTAIN); funded projects run through December 2026.

    Dr. Phan received the 2022 University of Memphis College of Arts and Sciences’ Distinguished Teaching Award.

  5. 2022

    Best Full Paper Award

    “Addressing Competing Objectives in Allocating Funds to Scholarships and Need-based Financial Aid” received the Best Full Paper Award at the 15th International Conference on Educational Data Mining (EDM 2022). Written with Laura Wright and Bridgette Decent, the paper addresses how to divide a limited budget between scholarships and need-based aid when the goals compete. An extended version, “Optimizing Financial Aid Allocation to Improve Access and Affordability to Higher Education,” appeared in the Journal of Educational Data Mining.

  6. 2018

  7. 2016–2018

    TigerCrew

    Supported by a $19,000 University of Memphis Strengthening Communities grant, TigerCrew brought together undergraduate students, CodeCrew (a non-profit organization), and the Public Library of Memphis to teach high school students to create video games.
